## What is ARSA?

ARSA is a music player for Android that streams the entire YouTube Music catalog — **no ads, no account, no paywalls**. It's built around a signature **Liquid Glass** interface with real-time frosted glass, spring physics, and artwork-reactive theming that makes your music feel alive.

Zero telemetry. Everything stays on your device.

## Install

> **Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher required.**

1. Go to **[download.app.aryavl.com](https://download.app.aryavl.com)**
2. Tap **Download APK**
3. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**
4. If prompted, enable *"Install from unknown sources"* for your browser

You may need to grant ARSA storage permission on first launch to access local files.

## FAQ

**Is it safe to sideload?**
Only ever install APKs from [download.app.aryavl.com](https://download.app.aryavl.com) or this GitHub Releases page — never from third-party mirrors.

**Why isn't it on the Play Store?**
YouTube's Terms of Service prohibit ad-free YouTube Music clients on the Play Store. ARSA is distributed directly.

**Is it free forever?**
Yes. ARSA will always be free to download and use. No subscriptions, no premium tier.

**How do I report a bug?**
Open an [Issue](https://github.com/AryaVLcom/A.R.S.A-Audio-Resonant-Streaming-Architecture-/issues) or join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Ejeb4cmzfd).
